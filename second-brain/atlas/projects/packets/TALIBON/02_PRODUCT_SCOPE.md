# Talibon Product and Scope

The core SSOT defines one municipal operating platform rather than unrelated CRUD modules. Shared primitives include identity, organization, employee, office, workflow, assignment, document, property, calendar, notification and audit. Public citizen systems remain a controlled external/integration lane unless explicitly re-scoped.
