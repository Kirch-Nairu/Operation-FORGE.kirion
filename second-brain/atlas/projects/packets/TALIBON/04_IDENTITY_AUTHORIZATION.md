# Talibon Identity and Authorization

Authorization is contextual: role, office, assignment, transaction type, workflow state, delegation and classification may all matter. Hidden UI controls are never sufficient security. Privileged exposure, protected employee/medical content and cross-office routing require server-side policy and auditable denial where appropriate.
