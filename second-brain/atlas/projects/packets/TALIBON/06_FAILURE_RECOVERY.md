# Talibon Failure and Recovery

Municipal workflow failure can strand transactions, deadlines, access changes, records, property accountability and executive decisions. Recovery therefore includes authoritative state, protected documents, queue/notification behavior, reassignment, backup/restore and proof that restored workflows remain internally consistent.
