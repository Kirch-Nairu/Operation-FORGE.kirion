## Semantic mesh

The global graph is intentionally **concept-centric**. Atomic notes under `mesh/` form dense neighborhoods around architecture, security, data, operations, reliability, governance and AI. Projects and operational systems connect into those concepts rather than sitting in isolated spokes.

Start with any mesh concept and open Local Graph at depth 2–3 to inspect its engineering neighborhood.
