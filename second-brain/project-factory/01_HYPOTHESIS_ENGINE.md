# Hypothesis Engine

Given a raw idea, infer the most likely useful V1.

## Output

- likely product identity
- first user
- one daily workflow
- MVP scope
- parked scope
- risky assumptions
- questions needed

## Hypothesis quality

A good hypothesis is narrow, testable, and cheap.

Bad:

```text
Build full business automation with dashboard, mobile, cloud, AI, SMS, and reports.
```

Good:

```text
Build a local-first tool for one daily workflow, with reports parked until transactions are stable.
```

Never treat a hypothesis as approval.
