# Autonomous Work Loop

1. Anchor repo truth.
2. Plan lane scope.
3. Map files and patterns.
4. Implement coherent changes.
5. Verify required gates.
6. Repair within scope.
7. Re-run gates.
8. Check for drift.
9. Write handoff.
10. Stop at CANDIDATE_AUTOMATED_PROVEN_MANUAL_PENDING or BLOCKED.

## No babysitting rule

Do not stop after every small edit.
Continue until a real blocker, failed unrecoverable gate, or manual-only boundary.
