# Research Protocol

## Start

- question
- current hypothesis
- why it matters
- decision/assumption affected
- acceptable source/evidence types
- time/cost boundary

## During

- separate source claims from inference
- preserve disagreement
- record recency/version when relevant
- prefer primary/authoritative sources for standards and vendor behavior
- do not turn a search result into proof without reading the source

## Stop

Stop when:
- evidence is sufficient for the decision's risk level
- further research has diminishing decision value
- a real experiment/test is cheaper than more reading
- the decision should be deferred rather than guessed

## Output

`SUPPORTED`, `DISPROVEN`, `INCONCLUSIVE`, or `CONTESTED`, with evidence strength.
