# Research Index

Research notes are evidence-oriented inquiries. They should link to the decision, assumption, threat, or architecture question they inform.

Views:
- open research questions
- research blocking decisions
- disproven hypotheses
- contested external evidence
- completed research promoted to patterns/decisions
