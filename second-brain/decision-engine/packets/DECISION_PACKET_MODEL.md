# Decision Packet Model

A material software decision is treated as a packet rather than a single rationale paragraph. The packet should make it possible to answer: what are we deciding, why, based on what evidence, what can fail, what can be exploited, what changes elsewhere, how do we mitigate, how do we verify, what does it cost, how reversible is it, and when must we reconsider?
