## Operating loop

```text
UNDERSTAND
→ PLAN
→ DECIDE
→ IMPLEMENT
→ VERIFY
→ OPERATE
→ OBSERVE
→ LEARN
→ REPLAN
```

The root provides high-level routing into the major sectors. Cross-sector continuity is expressed through genuine bridge concepts and shared governed objects, not through decorative hub-to-hub edges. This keeps native Obsidian Graph View structurally meaningful while preserving the underlying engineering model.

## Root laws

1. Reality outranks memory.
2. Working state, durable knowledge, evidence, decisions, risks, and scenarios remain separate object classes.
3. Rigor scales with consequence, reversibility, blast radius, and uncertainty.
4. Process must reduce uncertainty or coordination cost; otherwise remove it.
5. Semantic relationships must represent real dependencies, not decorative graph edges.

## Deep navigation

Detailed planning engines, decision trees, incident systems, patterns, risks, scenarios, projects, and reusable concepts are reached through their sector hubs rather than directly from the root. This keeps the central node useful instead of turning it into a graph black hole.
