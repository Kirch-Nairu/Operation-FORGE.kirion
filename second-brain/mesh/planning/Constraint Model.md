# Constraint Model

Constraints are conditions the solution must respect: budget, schedule, team capability, regulation, platform limits, data residency, offline requirements, compatibility, operational capacity, and irreversible commitments.

A constraint is useful only when its source, consequence, flexibility, and review trigger are known.
