# Dependency Map

A dependency map records what the work depends on and what depends on the work. Dependencies may be technical, organizational, contractual, infrastructure, data, vendor, approval, or timing constraints.

The map distinguishes blocking dependencies from optional integrations and identifies failure ownership.
