# Logging Baseline

Material systems record authentication and authorization events, privileged changes, security-control failures, important state transitions, deployment/configuration changes, and diagnostic context without exposing secrets or uncontrolled sensitive data. Logs use stable identifiers and retention appropriate to incident and operational needs.
