# CISA Secure by Design

CISA's Secure by Design guidance places security responsibility on technology producers rather than customers. Its three recurring principles are: take ownership of customer security outcomes, embrace radical transparency and accountability, and lead from the top. The practical direction favors secure defaults and eliminating vulnerability classes at design time.

Source: https://www.cisa.gov/securebydesign
