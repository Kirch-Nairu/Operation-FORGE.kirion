# Cryptographic Engineering System

Cryptographic engineering uses well-vetted primitives to protect confidentiality, integrity, authenticity, and key authority while making algorithms, keys, rotation, storage, failure, recovery, and migration explicit. Cryptography does not compensate for broken authorization or unsafe key custody.
