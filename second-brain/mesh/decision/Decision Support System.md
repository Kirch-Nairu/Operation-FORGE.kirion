# Decision Support System

A decision is not merely a choice. It is a governed packet of context, alternatives, evidence, tradeoffs, risks, failure modes, vulnerabilities, mitigations, verification obligations, reversibility, and revisit triggers.

## Required dimensions
- problem and decision question
- alternatives and rejected options
- supporting evidence and uncertainty
- architecture and operational consequences
- failure and vulnerability surface
- mitigations and hardening obligations
- verification and production observation
- reversibility and review triggers
