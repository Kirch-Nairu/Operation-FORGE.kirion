# Delivery Risk Premium

A delivery risk premium prices uncertainty or consequence that materially increases the probability or cost of rework: ambiguous requirements, unstable dependencies, inherited legacy state, weak access to stakeholders, security exposure, hard migration deadlines, or irreversible integration.
