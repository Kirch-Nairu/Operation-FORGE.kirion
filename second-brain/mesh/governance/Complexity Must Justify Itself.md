# Complexity Must Justify Itself

> Complexity must pay rent in capability, risk reduction, or operational leverage.

Every abstraction, service, dependency, workflow, queue, cache, configuration branch, role, and process adds failure modes and cognitive cost. Complexity is accepted deliberately and periodically re-evaluated.
