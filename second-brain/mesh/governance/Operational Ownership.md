# Operational Ownership

> If nobody owns failure, the architecture is incomplete.

Every important runtime, dependency, backup, alert, deployment, secret, security control, and recovery procedure needs an owner or explicit responsibility boundary. Operational ownership is part of design, not a post-launch staffing concern.
