# Engineering Doctrine

Engineering doctrine is the reusable set of principles that govern how uncertainty, complexity, authority, security, recoverability, evidence, quality, and operations are handled across projects. Doctrine guides decisions but yields to stronger project-specific evidence when context demands it.
