# Salryn Product Truth

Salryn is an offline-first Windows POS for MSMEs, built by Kirjane Labs.

Daily operation must not depend on internet.

## Known baseline

- C#/.NET backend
- WebView/Vite-style frontend
- SQLite local database
- local receipts
- inventory movement
- licensing lanes
- POS milestones
- eventual Salryn Standard skins and Business Suite

## Product principle

Salryn is not SaaS-first. Salryn is local-first.

Cloud, sync, mobile, eSales, BIR/RMO production posting, online payments, and external integrations are separate lanes unless explicitly approved.

## Standard skins

Salryn Standard can become parent source for:

- Salryn Retail
- Salryn Services
- Salryn Food
- Salryn Rooms
- Salryn Lending
- Salryn Payroll
- Salryn Ledger
- Salryn Business Suite

Each skin must pass Project Factory intake before implementation.
