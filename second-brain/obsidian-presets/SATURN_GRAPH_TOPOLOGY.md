# Saturn Graph Topology

This is a **navigation and visualization topology**, not a new authority layer.

Project Second Brain now has two complementary visual projections:

1. **Native Global Graph** — emergent, force-directed, useful for discovering unexpected semantic density and connectivity.
2. **Saturn Brain** — deterministic, read-only, purpose-built projection with a stable planet/ring/moon silhouette.

The native graph is never forced into a literal illustration by inventing links. The Saturn Brain view supplies the designed geometry while consuming the same real vault notes and resolved wiki-link relationships.

## Shape contract

### Inner planet

- `cognitive-os/CENTRAL_BRAIN.md` is the visual center.
- other Cognitive OS notes occupy the inner planet.
- these notes remain semantically authoritative only according to their existing authority metadata.

### Major sector ring

Eight major engineering sector hubs form the principal ring:

- Architecture
- Data
- Platform & Reliability
- Security
- Assurance & Learning
- Decision & Governance
- AI & Automation
- Projects

Only the Central Brain and major sector hubs receive accent colors. Atomic knowledge stays neutral.

### Ring constellations

Planning hubs and atomic concepts are projected into deterministic sector arcs. Their placement does not change their Markdown links or authority.

### Moons

Project notes are placed on an outer orbit. They remain ordinary project notes and can be opened directly from the visual projection.

## Semantic edge rule

Saturn Brain draws only relationships resolved from the vault's actual wiki links and frontmatter links. Decorative edges are forbidden.

## Native graph edge discipline

- Central Brain: inner core + sector routers only.
- Sector hub: Central Brain + previous sector + next sector + local constellation.
- Atomic concept: prefer 2 same-domain links + 1 parent/hub + at most 1 meaningful cross-domain bridge.
- Project: prefer 1 sector/planning anchor + 2–4 real concept links + material risk/decision links when they exist.
- Incident/lesson: affected project + relevant control/concept + resulting lesson/pattern.

No link may exist only to make the graph look better.

## Deterministic view interaction

The `second-brain-saturn` Obsidian plugin provides:

- stable Saturn geometry independent of force simulation;
- real semantic edges;
- hover-to-highlight neighbors;
- click-to-open notes;
- pan and cursor-centered zoom;
- fit, label, edge, and refresh controls;
- a permanently dark, restrained space aesthetic;
- accent color only on major hubs.

## Degradation guard

Aesthetic shaping must never remove required authority, evidence, decision, verification, recovery, or project context. The deterministic Saturn view never writes to vault notes. Repository/runtime truth continues to outrank every visualization.
