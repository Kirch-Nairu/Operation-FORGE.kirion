# Research Planning Hub

Research exists to reduce a named uncertainty before a decision or implementation step.

## Start with

- question
- why the answer matters
- current hypothesis
- decision blocked or influenced
- evidence required
- stop condition for research

## Research outcomes

```text
SUPPORTED
DISPROVEN
INCONCLUSIVE
CONTESTED
NOT_APPLICABLE
```

Research does not continue merely because more information exists. Stop when additional evidence is unlikely to change the decision enough to justify the cost.
